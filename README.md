# Wall Display Hooks

## Overview
These are fully 3D printable display hooks designed entirely by myself on Onshape. This is a very simple project that has a lot of functional usage, as well as being highly presentable. I enjoy decorating my space, and I also collect vinyl records as well as comic books. These hooks would allow anyone in my position to have an easy and efficient way to display their own collectible items! 

## Description
There are 5 different printable files on this repo. They are clearly labeled each by what they display-
Record case, 1/4th inch thick (1-4th Case)
Record case, 1/8th inch thick (1-8th Case)
Record case, 3/16ths inch thick (3-16ths Case)
Standalone vinyl without a case (Standalone Vinyl)
Comic book, with some extra room for a backing board (Comic Book)

WARNING: If you're using the standalone vinyl hooks, I would recommend putting a layer of foam or fabric in between the PLA and the record to avoid scratching or damaging it. 

## Explanation
Download the STL files from the repo, and print! Each STL has 4 hooks of the same type, each one angled the correct direction. Supports will be needed unless you print the STL on its side, unfortunately these do not have built in supports. Once you have your hooks printed, feel free to gently sand or otherwise polish them to get the visual appeal you want. When your hooks are ready, you can either drill through the back, use glue, poster tack, or poster tape. 

## Tips & Tricks
I recommend using poster tape or glue, but really it depends on your walls. Measure your record cases' depth to determine which size to print. Measure your item to determine how far apart the hooks should be. Once the hooks are attached, the item should slide along the middle as a track. Again, if you are using the standalone vinyl hooks, put a layer of foam or fabric between the hook and the record to avoid damaging them.
